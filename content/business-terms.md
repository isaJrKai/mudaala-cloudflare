## 1. What Mudaala provides

Mudaala helps people discover local businesses and the products or services they offer. A business can publish its profile, share its Mudaala link or QR code, and receive enquiries through the contact options available on its profile.

Mudaala is a discovery and contact service, not a party to a transaction between a business and a customer. Unless Mudaala explicitly announces a feature in the app, Mudaala does not take payment for your transaction, hold customer funds, guarantee bookings, arrange delivery, or guarantee sales or a particular number of enquiries.

## 2. Accurate business information

You agree to:
- Use a business name and contact details you are entitled to use.
- Keep your location, opening hours, descriptions, prices and service areas reasonably accurate and up to date.
- Publish genuine photos and only content you own or have permission to use.
- Make it clear when a price is an estimate, a starting price, negotiable, or available only after a quote.
- Respond honestly when customers ask about availability, prices, qualifications or the scope of work.

Do not impersonate another business, invent reviews or credentials, misrepresent stock or availability, or use Mudaala to mislead customers.

Businesses may offer physical products, services, or both where the relevant options are available in the app.

For a service listing, describe what is included, where you provide the service, and any important limits or conditions. If the final price depends on the job, use a quote or enquiry approach rather than presenting an uncertain amount as a guaranteed price.

Service providers remain responsible for performing their work safely and professionally, agreeing appointment times directly with customers, and communicating cancellations or changes. Publishing a service does not mean Mudaala has inspected, endorsed or certified the provider.

Mudaala does not currently promise that a business profile or listing has been independently verified merely because it appears on the platform.

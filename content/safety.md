# Safety Guide

Most trades go fine. These habits keep it that way.

## The three habits

1. **Meet in a public place.** A market, a petrol station, outside a shop, somewhere with people around, in daylight.
2. **Check the goods before you pay.** Count, weigh, switch on, try on. What you are holding is what you are buying.
3. **Never pay in advance.** Deposits before you have seen the goods, "delivery fees" to a stranger, mobile money to "confirm" a buyer. These are the classic scams. Pay when you hold the goods.

## For buyers

- Compare the price with similar ads on Mudaala. If it looks too good, it is a hook.
- Ask for the shop code (every Mudaala seller has one) and look at their shop page before you travel.
- Keep chats inside WhatsApp or normal calls; do not move to secret apps.

## For sellers

- Meet buyers the same way: public place, cash or mobile money confirmed on your own phone before you hand over goods.
- "I will send a rider to collect" plus a partial payment is a known trick. Wait until the money is really in your account.
- Beware of cheques. Cheques can bounce after you have handed over the goods.

## Reporting a bad ad

Every listing and shop page has a **Report** button. Reporting takes seconds, you do not need an account, and you can stay anonymous to the seller. When several different people report the same listing, it is hidden automatically while the team reviews it.

If you have been scammed, also report to the police. If a listing involves stolen goods, especially copper, cables or other public infrastructure, report it: those sales hurt everyone in the community.

## If something goes wrong

Save the other person's number, the ad link and any messages. Evidence makes reports strong. Reach the team through the support email in your notifications for help with appeals or a report that was missed.

# Mudaala Brand Integrity Standard

**Status:** Founder-directed product-wide quality standard  
**Applies to:** Every Mudaala surface, including the public marketplace, merchant workspace, account flows, product creation, dashboards, notifications, Telegram Mini App, posters, PWA, and future interfaces.

## Non-negotiable principle

Mudaala must look and behave like one intentionally designed product. Remove generic AI-generated styling, stock-template layouts, decorative filler, borrowed identities, and components that do not serve a real user need.

## Brand foundations

- Use the approved Mudaala market-stall M mark and official horizontal/stacked lockups from the brand pack. Do not use the retired leaf mark or add a black circular backing, decorative ring, or unapproved treatment around the logo.
- Preserve the established forest-green, mango/marigold, warm-paper, ink, and restrained textile palette.
- Keep the product grounded in local Ugandan commerce: real shops, real products, useful seller information, clear prices and units, and direct ways to contact or visit sellers.
- Use real product/shop photography where available. Never fabricate merchants, products, testimonials, activity, trust signals, or imagery presented as real.
- Typography, spacing, geometry, surfaces, and motion must be consistent and purposeful. Avoid effects added only to make a screen look "AI-designed".

## Remove or redesign

- Generic iconography used as a substitute for Mudaala's identity.
- Generic gradient/glassmorphism treatments, neon accents, excessive shadows, floating decorative shapes, and gratuitous 3D.
- Empty dashboard metrics, fake charts, invented activity, placeholder content shipped as finished UI, and meaningless badges.
- Repeated card grids or oversized hero sections that bury the task a merchant or buyer came to complete.
- Unnecessary labels, duplicate navigation, redundant buttons, and features presented without a clear user outcome.
- Animations that do not communicate state, feedback, or a useful transition.
- Stock copy and generic AI phrases that do not sound like Mudaala or help the user act.

## Design and implementation gates

Every new or revised screen must pass these questions before merge:

1. Does it use the correct Mudaala identity and visual language?
2. Does every visible element help a buyer or merchant understand, decide, or act?
3. Is the content truthful and grounded in actual app data?
4. Does it work on a small phone, with clear hierarchy and readable contrast?
5. Are loading, empty, error, and success states deliberate and useful?
6. Is motion restrained, accessible, and reduced-motion safe?
7. Does it reuse existing Mudaala components and data flows instead of introducing a parallel or generic implementation?

If an element fails these checks, remove it or redesign it before release. Do not add decoration to compensate for weak information architecture.

## Audit rule

Treat this as a continuous product-wide audit, not a one-time Mini App styling task. Review existing screens in coherent slices, record concrete findings, fix the source components, and verify each change. Do not delete useful functionality merely because it is visually plain; preserve the user outcome and redesign its presentation.
